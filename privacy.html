DrawScope Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

1. Introduction

DrawScope provides historical lottery-result analysis, digit-pattern analysis, sum analysis, and statistical tools for Pick 3, Daily 4, and related lottery games. This policy explains how the app handles information.

2. Personal Information

The current version of DrawScope does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number to use its analysis features. Based on the current implementation, the developer has not identified a feature designed to collect personal information through these analysis tools.

3. Lottery Data and On-Device Processing

Historical lottery results are bundled with the app. Analysis features use these results to calculate patterns, sums, frequencies, and other statistical summaries. The app is designed to use local historical data when its optional local API endpoint is unavailable.

4. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Review of the current project did not identify third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. DrawScope is not designed to track users across other companies' apps and websites. Features and data practices may change in future versions.

5. Device Permissions

The current iOS permission checks did not identify requests for access to location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

6. Data Retention and Security

DrawScope does not require an account for its current analysis features. No software or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device and operating system up to date.

7. Children's Privacy

DrawScope is not designed to collect personal information from children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

8. Third-Party Services

This policy covers the DrawScope app. The support website and any third-party websites you visit are subject to their own privacy practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this address.

10. Contact

For support or questions, visit the DrawScope Support website or the GitHub support and feedback page .
